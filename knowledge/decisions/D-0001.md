# D-0001 — A timer fuse, a bounce instead of a break, half a TNT, the game's own explosion

**Decision**: The bundle is a `ThrowableItemProjectile` whose fuse is a
timer from the throw (40 ticks) and not an impact trigger: it bounces off
blocks and entities (a third of the speed into the surface back out, under
half of the slide kept) and comes to rest on a floor below a small speed,
hissing every ten ticks, then explodes where it lies with the game's own
explosion at power 2 and the TNT interaction (blocks drop at TNT's decay
rate, no fire), or with no block interaction when the server turns block
breaking off. Thrown at a snowball's speed with a ten-tick cooldown; a
dispenser throws it through `ProjectileItem`. Crafted shapeless from three
gunpowder, two paper and a string. Three sounds of its own cut from CC0
recordings; the blast is vanilla's.

**Why**: Rusty: "TNT blocks are too strong and too unwieldy ... sometimes
you just want to clear out a little area with blasting ... a little
three-stick bundle of dynamite that you can throw and it explodes after a
short delay ... functionally like weaker TNT that you can throw like a
projectile". A timer fuse is what "explodes after a short delay" means, and
it is what makes the thing useful: it can be lobbed into a hole or over a
wall and go off there. Power 2 is half of TNT's 4: about a three-block
reach through air and a shallow crater, a little area. Reusing the game's
explosion keeps the sound, the drops and the damage everyone already
knows. The bounce keeps under half of the slide because a paper bundle on
grass skidded across the whole booth at more.

**Alternatives rejected**: *Exploding on impact* -- a grenade that cannot
be placed. *A custom explosion* -- nothing about a smaller TNT needs one.
*Sticking where it lands* -- physical enough to bounce, and the bounce is
the fun.
