# Sound sources

Every sound the mod plays is cut by `devtools/art/build.py` from one of the
recordings below, kept here as downloaded (freesound's HQ preview). All three
are released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication), confirmed
on each sound's page on the day it was taken; no attribution is required,
and it is given anyway.

| File | Cut from | By | Freesound | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fuse.ogg` | "S31-06 Fuse burning.wav" | craigsmith | https://freesound.org/s/675770/ | CC0 1.0 |
| `throw.ogg` | "Fast whoosh, bamboo swoosh through air, version 2" | zapsplat.com | https://freesound.org/s/719637/ | CC0 1.0 |
| `land.ogg` | "Wooden Thud (Mono)" | Breviceps | https://freesound.org/s/449955/ | CC0 1.0 |

The blast is the game's own `entity.generic.explode`, played by the explosion.
